# A2.b.vi inactive schedule controls

Vendor Connect now reads the server's versioned schedule/history for the selected saved account and list. Owners and managers may edit weekdays, local time, IANA time zone and grace period, then save an **off** configuration. Concurrent edits use the expected server version. Missed, pending, in-progress, unknown, received, saved and unchanged are shown separately.

The enable control is deliberately blocked until the signed-in open-tab due-check and actual import-result completion are wired and verified. Existing manual Refresh prices remains available. When the schedule migrations are absent, the panel remains hidden; no schedule is silently activated. This slice changes no companion extension and contacts no supplier.
